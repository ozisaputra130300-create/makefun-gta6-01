SOLMAR — an open-world crime story in a sun-soaked coastal city. You are Danny "Dex" Marlowe, a wheelman who owes $50,000 to loan shark Sal Bianchi. Explore Solmar on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police, and work off the debt across four missions: The First Job ($500), Getaway Driver ($1,000), The Pickup ($900), and The Big Score — a $5,000 bank vault job.

The city is alive: traffic and pedestrians fill the streets, and a 0–5 star wanted level brings the Solmar PD after you — get busted or wasted and you lose 10% of your cash. Fight on foot, grab health, ammo and cash pickups, and watch the HUD's minimap, wanted stars and mission marker. Progress and money are saved in the browser, so you can pick up where you left off.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. The game is static files under `game/` with no build step and no network requests.

### Controls

- **WASD** — move on foot / drive (W/S forward and back, A/D steer)
- **E** — steal a car / get out / start a mission
- **F** — punch
- **Click** — shoot
- **P** — pause (progress saves)
- **Space** — start

Follow the yellow marker to your next job.
